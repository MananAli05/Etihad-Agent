# Etihad Garden Overview
Etihad Garden is a premier master-planned residential and commercial housing society located in Rahim Yar Khan, Pakistan. It offers modern urban living, state-of-the-art infrastructure, secure gated community living, underground utilities, wide carpeted roads, and lush green parks designed for luxury and comfort.

# Location
- **City:** Rahim Yar Khan, Punjab, Pakistan.
- **Address / Key Location:** Main Airport Road / Main Boulevard, Rahim Yar Khan.
- **Nearby Connectivity Highlights:**
  - Sheikh Zayed Hospital & Medical College (approx. 5 minutes)
  - Rahim Yar Khan Airport (approx. 8-10 minutes)
  - City Commercial Hub (approx. 5-7 minutes)
